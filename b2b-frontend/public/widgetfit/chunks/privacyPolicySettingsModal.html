Privacy Policy
Google collects user data to make our services more convenient. This not only helps us determine what language you are using, but also more complex tasks, such as automatically matching you with useful ads or interesting YouTube videos, and helping you find friends and acquaintances online. The type of data we collect depends on how you use our products and what privacy settings are set in your account.

If you are not signed in to your Google Account, we store the information we collect using unique identifiers associated with your browsers, applications and devices. This allows us to remember your preferences and other data, such as your preferred language, ad personalization settings, and search results.

If you have logged in, we collect and store information in your Google Account that we process as personal data.
